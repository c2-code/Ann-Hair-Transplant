Services / Hair Transplant

Hair Transplant

Moving your own healthy follicles to the areas where hair has thinned, planned around your hairline, donor supply and long-term pattern.

The procedure

How it works

Follicles are taken from a donor area, usually the back and sides of the scalp where hair tends to be more resistant to thinning. They're then placed into the recipient area at angles chosen to match natural growth.

The technique, graft count and hairline design are decided by the surgical team after evaluating your scalp. [Technique details to be provided by the Princess Ann team.]

Procedure videosurgical team footage, provided by clinic
Candidate considerations

Is it right for you?

Donor supply

You need enough healthy donor hair to cover the area you want to restore.

Stable pattern

Hair loss that's still progressing quickly can affect long-term planning.

Overall health

Some conditions and medications affect suitability. This is reviewed in consultation.

Recovery

A typical timeline

WhenWhat's common
First daysRedness, tenderness and some swelling. Small scabs form around grafts.
Weeks 1 to 2Scabs fall away. Most people return to routine activities, following clinic instructions.
Weeks 2 to 8Transplanted hairs often shed. This is expected and is not graft loss.
Months 3 to 6New growth begins to appear, often fine at first.
Months 12 to 18Growth matures and the final result becomes clearer.

Timelines vary by person. Follow your surgical team's aftercare instructions.

Risks

What to know before you decide

Like any surgical procedure, hair transplantation carries risks. These can include bleeding, infection, swelling, temporary numbness, folliculitis, temporary shedding of existing hair, visible scarring, and results that are less dense than hoped. Your surgical team will review risks specific to you before any procedure. Results are not guaranteed.

Services / Eyebrow Transplant

Eyebrow Transplant

Rebuilding sparse, thin or uneven brows with your own hair, placed one follicle at a time to follow natural direction.

Eyebrow procedure photoprovided by clinic
The procedure

Precision over volume

Eyebrows use far fewer grafts than the scalp, but each one matters more. Follicles are taken from a donor area and placed at a shallow angle to match how brows grow.

Shape and density are planned with you before the procedure. [Graft range and pricing for eyebrows TBC.]

Good to know

Who it suits

People with brows thinned by over-plucking, scarring, or naturally sparse growth. Suitability is confirmed in consultation.

Ongoing care

Transplanted hairs come from the scalp and may keep growing longer, so regular trimming is usually needed.

Recovery

Mild redness and scabbing are common early. Shedding and regrowth follow a timeline similar to scalp transplants.

Risks include swelling, infection, scarring and uneven growth. Results vary and are not guaranteed.
